  • Bids stay encrypted while bidding is open. When the sale ends they are ranked from highest to lowest, and the lowest winning bid, the one that fills the last NFT, sets the price every winner pays. Extra cZAMA is refunded.
  • Bids above the final price get their full quantity. Bids exactly at the final price split the remaining NFTs pro rata. Bids below it get nothing and are refunded.
  • If total demand stays below the number of NFTs for sale, everyone buys at the lowest bid price.
  • Bids are binding, and each one carries a small non-refundable fee for encrypted bidding. The fee amount is not modeled here.
  • The site does not describe how pro rata shares are rounded. This tool rounds down and gives leftover NFTs to the largest remainders, so a real result may differ by an NFT.
